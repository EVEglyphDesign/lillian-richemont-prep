# SAP CS equipment-master coverage across Richemont Maisons

Standing question: is the SAP Customer Service (CS) module — with equipment masters
keyed to caseback serial numbers — a Cartier / IWC thing, or is it a **Group template
that spans the Maisons**?

Answer: **it's a Group template**, deployed as a shared repair platform under
Richemont Group Technology, with the Maisons on it as tenants. The public-website
warranty portals (Cartier Care, My IWC, and so on) are the customer-facing skin over
the same underlying SAP CS surface.

## The Group-level evidence

Two Richemont insiders describe the template in their own words.

**Guillaume Devif** — Team Manager, SAP Retail, CRM & CS at Richemont, Geneva,
2010–2017 — says he was
[in charge of SAP CS, Retail and CRM support team for the whole European scope](https://www.linkedin.com/in/guillaume-devif-3887521b),
"to maintain the solution and make it adopted by end users (150+ luxury boutiques,
**10 repair workshops**, 10 CRM managers of different Maisons)" and "Responsible for
deploying the **template solution**, in collaboration with development teams."
"European Roll out" is called out by name.

**Andrea Ghirardo** — SAP CS Senior Product Specialist at Richemont, April 2024 to
present, in [Richemont Group Technology dept](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andreaghirardo)
— "supporting new initiatives and ongoing business in the Customer Services area with
focus on **SAP ECC** system and integration with other SAP and non-SAP systems and
applications." His declared SAP CS skill set spells the objects out:
"**Warranty Management, Technical Objects (Vehicles, Equipments, Serial Numbers,
Measuring Points, etc.), ADP component**."

That is the shape of the template. It is on ECC (not S/4), it manages equipment
records and serial numbers, and it drives warranty management across a shared repair
network — the same "10 repair workshops" number Devif quotes.

The Group is currently
[hiring an SAP CS/SD/Retail Consultant](https://talents.vaia.com/companies/richemont/sap-cs-sd-retail-consultant-37973497/)
in Bellevue, Switzerland — CS is being staffed today, not decommissioned.

## What this means in the interview

- The template is **one SAP CS instance covering most Maisons**, with the customer
  portals (Cartier Care, My IWC, VC's blockchain passport via Watchfinder) as the
  differentiated skin. That is the shape of most Group templates at Richemont, and
  it is what Lillian should assume when asked "does each Maison have its own?"
- The unusual data-layer question is **equipment masters keyed to caseback serials**
  driving eight-year warranty extensions and multi-decade service records. That is
  the exact SAP CS pattern (`EQUI` table, IQ01 / IE01 transactions, serial number
  profile on material master), and it produces the client-lifetime record that
  Richemont's business model depends on.
- The stack is still **ECC** on the CS side as of 2024 (Ghirardo's stated system).
  That is a live migration lane — RISE with SAP was signed for Group in 2023 (per
  [SAP news](https://news.sap.com/africa/2023/05/richemont-harmonizing-supply-chain-processes-between-group-business-and-suppliers/)),
  and CS on ECC will need to move to S/4HANA Service one day. The equipment-master
  data model changes materially in that move.
- The **repair-workshop count of ten** (Devif's number) is the real physical
  footprint of the template — Fort Worth, Mississauga, Sydney, London, and the
  European workshops. That is the customer-service backbone. Data-layer projects at
  Richemont end up touching this network more often than they touch retail POS,
  because the client-lifetime asset is the servicing record, not the initial sale.
