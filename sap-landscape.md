# Richemont SAP Landscape — Research Brief

*Prepared from public sources only. Every claim below is sourced inline. Anchor facts already known are not re-derived here.*

## 1. How many SAP instances, and how are they organized?

The only hard, sourced instance count remains the **~120 SAP instances** (alongside ~5,000 virtual machines) migrated to AWS by end-2022, disclosed in Richemont's own [AWS partnership announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211122005617/en/Richemont-Selects-AWS-as-Its-Preferred-Cloud-Provider-to-Drive-Product-Innovation) and repeated in secondary analysis on [undisturbed.blog](https://undisturbed.blog/articles/richemont). Neither source breaks the 120 down by country, Maison, or environment (dev/QA/prod), and no more recent public figure updates this count.

On organization, the picture that emerges is **one central ERP core rolled out via a group template, deployed market-by-market/region-by-region, with Maison- and function-specific satellites layered on top** — not a strict single global instance, but not fully Maison-siloed either. Richemont's own FY2021 annual report describes a **single "ERP platform"** being extended market by market, citing the "successful roll-out of Richemont's ERP platform in China," achieved by the **Asia Pacific regional team**, with plans to "continue the extension of its ERP platform to other markets" ([Richemont Annual Report business review](https://www.richemont.com/media/sk3jorno/2-business-review-p-6-to-32.pdf)). A LinkedIn profile of a Richemont SAP Logistics lead independently confirms template governance below the ERP-core level: she describes herself as "**SAP Logistics template authority for Richemont's Regional Distribution Centers**, leading a 6–10 person team" ([Esha Gupta, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/esha-gupta-4355491b)). A Delvaux integration consultant's profile likewise references "**roll-out of full SAP, POS and satellite business applications**" as part of onboarding acquired Maisons into "**Group's operating model and template**" ([Antonio Parisi, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antonioparisiprofile)). The current New Foundations job ads reinforce this: the programme exists to define "**a coherent, future-proof and sustainable enterprise landscape**" and explicitly separates "**ERP core**" from "**satellites, integrations and extensions**" on BTP and third-party platforms ([New Foundations Technical Manager JD, jobup.ch](https://www.jobup.ch/en/jobs/detail/40c23635-5261-44a5-aa63-12e4b37240be/)).

## 2. SAP modules and satellites beyond Ariba

Public evidence, mostly from Richemont employee/consultant LinkedIn profiles and SAP's own event materials, confirms a broad SAP footprint:

- **SuccessFactors / Concur**: A Richemont HRIS Manager Europe's LinkedIn history lists "**Project Lead Consultant – SAP HCM/HRIS/SuccessFactors & Concur**" work for Richemont ([Birgit Proffen, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/birgit-proffen-83927674)); a current Richemont HRIS Specialist job ad lists **SAP Time, SAP PT, SAP PY** plus Workday and SuccessFactors among "HRIS tools" in scope ([Richemont careers site](https://jobs.richemont.com/Richemont/job/Meyrin-HRIS-Specialist-GE/1201323301/)). SAP Concur Invoice was purchased for AP automation in 2015 per a technographics database ([AppsRunTheWorld](https://www.appsruntheworld.com/customers-database/customers/view/richemont-switzerland)).
- **BW / BW/4HANA, Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)**: A former Richemont SAP Analytics Senior Consultant describes "**support and enhancement of BI platforms (BW, Datasphere, SAC) for global reporting and analytics**" ([Mohamed K, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohamed-k-5a581262)); a Richemont "IT Manager – SAP Analytics & Planning" profile lists deep **BW** (extractors, BEx, process chains) and **BusinessObjects** experience ([José Boavida, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joseboavida)). SAP's own 2022 BTP roadshow deck confirms Richemont's Group CSR function uses **SAC** across **26 Maisons** for carbon-reporting, explicitly citing "**SEM will become obsolete**" as a stated pain point ([SAP customer case PDF](https://assets.dm.ux.sap.com/ch-btp-roadshow-2022-2/pdfs/customer_case_richemont.pdf)); a separate 2021 SAP Innovation Awards deck covers a parallel APAC Finance SAC rollout ([SAP Innovation Awards pitch deck](https://www.sap.com/bin/sapdxc/proxy.inmsl.attachment.12766.pitch-deck.pdf)).
- **SAP Commerce Cloud (Hybris)**: A Richemont-focused full-stack developer profile references building "**a centralized Omnichannel e-commerce platform**" and lists "**SAP Commerce Cloud Developer (Hybris) | Spartacus**" as a specialism ([Abdenour Teggour, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/teggourabdenour)); another Richemont omnichannel manager profile cites "**functional expertise of SAP hybris commerce and Adobe AEM**" ([Arianna Dambrosio, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/arianna-dambrosio-9b17654b)).
- **SAP Apparel & Footwear (IS-AFS) / Fashion Management**: A peer-reviewed technical paper co-authored by a Richemont employee (email domain richemont.com) is specifically about "**Enhancing Fashion Retail with SAP Fashion Management**," the successor product that merges **IS-AFS** with **SAP Retail** ([JSAER paper](https://jsaer.com/download/vol-7-iss-10-2020/JSAER2020-7-10-216-220.pdf)), indicating live use of apparel/footwear-specific SAP retail functionality.
- **SAP MDG (Master Data Governance)**: A current Richemont Americas job ad for a "Sr. Master Data Specialist" states the role will "**lead customer, pricing and vendor creation and modification requests through SAP MDG**" within the "**SAP ERP & S/4HANA environment**" ([Richemont job posting, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/sr-master-data-specialist-at-richemont-4417875033)).
- **SAP EWM**: Confirmed via the "SAP EWM & Logistics Tech Team Lead at Richemont" LinkedIn title cited above ([Esha Gupta, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/esha-gupta-4355491b)).
- **GRC, Datasphere, Analytics Cloud**: Datasphere and SAC are directly confirmed (above); no public source explicitly names SAP GRC/Access Control at Richemont — treat as unconfirmed.
- **Non-SAP satellites**: **Salesforce/Google Cloud (ELEVATE)** clienteling — confirmed extensively, including "**one unified global database, one marketing automation platform, one measurement framework**" per Group Client Marketing Director Philippe Meyer, covering roughly **10,000 users across 45 markets** ([Salesforce blog](https://www.salesforce.com/eu/blog/richemont-maisons-delight-customers/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/richemont-hires-key-roles-boost-client-centricity-elevate-meyer); [undisturbed.blog](https://undisturbed.blog/articles/richemont)). **Workday** — confirmed as a first-ever, board-approved group-wide HCM implementation "**across every Maison globally**," reported live in 2026 ([Phil Humphrey, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/phil-humphrey-4900998)), alongside active Workday-focused hiring ([Richemont job ad, trabajo.org](https://ch.trabajo.org/stellenangebot-5483-82a1078fc89039fa8f698b42d110bcaf)). **Cegid Y2 POS** — a retail systems consultant profile lists delivering "**full-lifecycle implementations of Retail Pro, Cegid Y2, and Odoo for clients including Richemont**" ([Sabtain Ul Hassan, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sabtain-ul-hassan)). **Centric PLM** is documented for YNAP specifically (from 2017, pre-Richemont-exit) via Centric's own case study ([Centric Software](https://www.centricsoftware.com/success-stories/yoox-net-a-porter/)), not confirmed group-wide for Richemont Maisons.

## 3. Pre-acquisition vs. acquired-in SAP systems

Evidence points to a repeated pattern: **acquired Maisons run legacy/local systems at close, then get migrated onto the Group SAP template as a discrete post-merger project.**

- **Buccellati** (acquired 2019, [Richemont press release](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-acquires-buccellati/)): a 2025 Richemont job ad for a Buccellati FP&A controller in Milan explicitly frames the work as supporting "**the implementation of the Group SAP project**" and "**Group SAP Rollout Support**" ([Wizbii job listing](https://en.wizbii.com/company/richemont/job/junior-controller-1y-fixed-term)) — i.e., Buccellati was *not* on the Group template at acquisition and was still being migrated onto it years later. Richemont's FY2021 annual report separately notes the **Asia Pacific regional team integrated Buccellati in China** as part of the ERP rollout there ([Richemont Annual Report](https://www.richemont.com/media/sk3jorno/2-business-review-p-6-to-32.pdf)).
- **Delvaux** (acquired June 2021, [Richemont press release](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-acquires-delvaux/)): its former Head of IT states she "**led the full integration of the Information Systems into the Group ecosystem**," overseeing a landscape that included "**SAP ERP, PLM, CRM, CMS, E-commerce**" and a "**strategic migration to AWS**" ([Annaïck Solleu, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anna%C3%AFcksolleu)) — implying Delvaux had its own pre-existing SAP ERP instance that was then folded into Group infrastructure/AWS rather than replaced outright. A consultant profile separately references "**Delvaux – global integration, 12 Countries**" work including "**AFS ECC integration model**" ([Antonio Parisi, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/antonioparisiprofile)).
- **Vhernier** (agreed May 2024, completed September 2024, [Richemont press releases](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-acquires-vhernier/); [completion notice](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-completes-the-acquisition-of-maison-vhernier/)) and **Watchfinder** (acquired 2018, [Richemont press release](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/richemont-acquires-watchfindercouk-limited/)): no public source describes their SAP/ERP integration specifics — a gap (see Section 5).
- **YNAP**: Richemont sold 100% of YNAP to Mytheresa, closing 24 April 2025, with the combined entity renamed **LuxExperience B.V.** ([Richemont/Mytheresa closing announcement](https://www.richemont.com/news-media/press-releases-news/myt-netherlands-parent-bv-mytheresa-and-richemont-announce-the-successful-completion-of-mytheresa-s-acquisition-of-yoox-net-a-porter-ynap/)). The deal terms explicitly state that "**YNAP's white label service business will be discontinued as soon as the Richemont Maisons' online stores powered by YNAP have been migrated to their own chosen platforms**," and that the merged group will "**share infrastructure including Mytheresa's technology platform**" going forward ([GlobeNewswire deal announcement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/10/07/2958643/0/en/myt-netherlands-parent-b-v-mytheresa-and-richemont-sign-agreement-for-mytheresa-to-acquire-yoox-net-a-porter-ynap-to-create-leading-global-multi-brand-digital-luxury-group-in-excha.html)). No public source states whether YNAP's own ERP/PLM stack (Centric PLM confirmed from 2017) was retained inside LuxExperience or replaced — this is unconfirmed (see gaps).

## 4. Template rollout approach and pain points

Richemont's own language across a decade of disclosure is consistent: **one Group ERP platform/template, extended progressively market-by-market and Maison-by-Maison**, not simultaneous global cutover. The FY2021 annual report frames this as ongoing multi-year work ("*extension of its ERP platform to other markets during the coming financial year*") ([Richemont Annual Report](https://www.richemont.com/media/sk3jorno/2-business-review-p-6-to-32.pdf)). New Foundations continues this logic for S/4HANA: the **Scope and Simplification Manager** job ad casts the role as reconciling "**Richemont's current operations**" against **S/4HANA standard processes**, explicitly working "**closely with the ECC Functional Expert to anchor recommendations in the reality of the current landscape**" — language that only makes sense if the "current landscape" is heterogeneous, ECC-based, and carries meaningful custom configuration ([New Foundations Scope & Simplification Manager JD](https://ch.trabajo.org/stellenangebot-1917-9b877a108f4d2ecdc6fe252cb63d3df2)). The same ad frames the mandate as "**guardian of simplicity, harmonisation and clean-core discipline**" and instructs "**keep / adapt / retire**" decisions on "**processes and custom code**" — a direct signal that accumulated ECC customization/complexity is a named pain point going into the transformation. The Technical Manager JD independently confirms the target state is "**SAP S/4HANA-centred**" with "**ERP core, satellites, integrations and extensions**" on BTP and third-party platforms, and stresses defining "**clear ERP boundaries**" between what's "**removed or simplified**" and what's "**rebuilt, extended or externalised**" ([New Foundations Technical Manager JD](https://www.jobup.ch/en/jobs/detail/40c23635-5261-44a5-aa63-12e4b37240be/)) — again implying today's landscape is sprawling enough to need active boundary-setting. On procurement specifically, Richemont's own USF 2022 conference testimony flags that "many aspects were poorly or not at all covered by our ERP" (contract management, strategic sourcing reporting, indirect spend) as the original 2015 rationale for layering Ariba/Business Network on top rather than extending ECC itself ([USF 2022 write-up, digital-achat.com](https://test.digital-achat.com/en/blog/articles/convention-usf-2022-richemont-presente-son-projet-aux-utilisateurs-sap/)).

## What is NOT publicly stated

- **Exact current instance count and its composition.** The 120-instance figure is from a 2021 AWS migration announcement (completed by end-2022) — no public source confirms whether this number has changed, how many are productive vs. non-productive, or how many are S/4HANA vs. still ECC today.
- **Whether New Foundations targets a single global S/4HANA instance or multiple regional/Maison instances.** Job ads describe an "ERP core" and "boundaries" but never state instance topology.
- **A confirmed go-live date or wave plan for the S/4HANA conversion itself** — job ads describe programme-setup roles (architecture, scope/simplification) rather than an announced cutover schedule.
- **Watchfinder's and Vhernier's SAP/ERP integration history** — no public source (press, LinkedIn, SI case study) describes what systems they ran pre-acquisition or how/whether they were migrated onto Richemont's SAP template.
- **Whether YNAP's Centric PLM and other systems were retained, replaced, or shared with Mytheresa's existing stack** post-April 2025 sale — deal announcements describe "shared infrastructure" in general terms only.
- **The identity and scope of the primary systems integrator(s) for New Foundations.** Capgemini, Deloitte, and Accenture all show individual consultants with Richemont SAP experience on LinkedIn, and Capgemini is confirmed for the separate R:Tech Lisbon hub, but no press release names a lead SI for the S/4HANA Bluefield conversion itself.
- **Confirmed use of SAP GRC/Access Control.** No public source names it directly at Richemont, despite its likely relevance to a multi-entity clean-core programme.
- **Quantified custom-code/Z-code volume in ECC** — job ads reference "keep/adapt/retire" custom-code decisions and clean-core discipline, but no number, percentage, or audit finding is public.
